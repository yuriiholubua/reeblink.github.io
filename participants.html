Why are Pioneers needed?

The standard is a set of programmable rules, executed by code: they enable independent properties and participants to interact with mutual trust in a single network — on top of local law, not instead of it.

By applying those rules in their own countries and jurisdictions, Pioneers deploy the global network: checking the rules against local law, activating the first properties, launching services, or renting a home.

Pioneers don't need to be real estate experts. A network needs many roles — there's one for everyone.
